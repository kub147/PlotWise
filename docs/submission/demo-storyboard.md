# Demo storyboard

One farmer, one field, one question: **"What if I had run this field in a different rotation for the last 30 years?"**

Two versions: a 30-second cut and a 2-minute walkthrough. Past Space Apps forms asked for a 30-second video or a short
slide deck; **check the length and format on the Project tab before recording.** Record after the feature freeze, from
the live app, in a 1920 × 1080 browser window at 100% zoom.

Every number below is what the app shows today for field **Tribune 001** at the default "Average field" yield level.
If the engine is re-run, re-check the numbers on screen before recording and read them from the screen, not from here.

## Before recording

- Open the app with `?field=ks071-001` so Tribune 001 is selected.
- On Rotation replay, press "Reset to equal weights".
- Close browser extensions and notifications; hide the bookmarks bar.
- Voice-over in plain English, no jargon. Say "greenness", not "NDVI"; "thirst of the air", not "reference ET".

## 2-minute walkthrough

| Step | Screen | Action | What to say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | My field | Start on Tribune 001. Point at the crop strip. | The tool read this field's history from USDA crop maps built on satellite images. The farmer typed nothing. The rotation today is wheat–sorghum–fallow. |
| 2 | My field | Scroll the card: soil, bare-ground days, greenness chart, heat image. | The soil can hold about 13 inches of water for plants. The ground is bare most of the year. Landsat shows every crop as a green peak; the grey band is bare ground. |
| 3 | What has changed | Show the four trend cards, then the rainfall card. | Each trend carries a label: reliable or not. The growing season is warmer and the air is thirstier. For rainfall, one NASA record shows drying and three other records do not, so we do not claim rain is declining. |
| 4 | What has changed | GRACE chart and the fallow-against-sorghum table. | From orbit, the region has lost about 7 inches of stored water since 2002. On the same August afternoon, bare fallow was about 10 °F hotter at the surface than a sorghum field. |
| 5 | Rotation replay | Read the warning at the top aloud. | These are comparisons, not farm profit: full costs with land rent, no crop insurance. |
| 6 | Rotation replay | Move "Net return" to 100 and the others to 0; then "Soil" to 100. Watch the first place change. | With equal priorities it is a close call. If only money counts, one rotation leads; if soil counts, another does. The tool tells you which, and why. |
| 7 | Rotation replay | Click wheat–sorghum–fallow; scroll its card; click an evidence ID. | Every card says why, shows the evidence, what you gain, what you risk, what we do not know, and what to check locally. Each evidence number opens the source. |
| 8 | Rotation replay | Switch to "Well managed". Then pick Hays 001 from My field and return. | Costs assume a well-managed field, so we show that level too. Move 230 km east to Hays, where it rains more, and replacing fallow starts to pay. |
| 9 | Region | Click two counties, west and east. | The same replay for 46 counties: the gain from replacing fallow rises from west to east. |
| 10 | This season | Show the percentile. | Soil moisture today is in the 96th percentile for this date. We can show where the soil stands. We cannot yet project the season from it, and the page says that. |
| 11 | How we know | Validation chart, the four tiles, "What we do not claim". | Sorghum is modelled usefully, wheat weakly, and the model finds the failure years. We list what we do not claim. |

## Lines to keep

- "It reads your field's history from space. You do not type it in."
- "Every bar is a real year."
- "It does not pick a winner. It shows when the answer depends on what you value."
- "Where the data cannot answer, it says so."

## Lines to avoid

- Any sentence that says rain is declining.
- "Profit", "you would have earned": say "return per acre in this comparison".
- "The best rotation": say "comes first under these priorities".
- Any forecast for next season.

## Slides, if the form wants slides instead of a video

Seven slides, one idea each, same order: the question · the field read from space · what has changed and what has
not · the replay and the sliders · the rotation card with evidence and gaps · how we know (validation) · the region
and what comes next. Use screenshots of the live app; no stock images.
